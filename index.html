Privacy Policy for Garage Hub

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Garage Hub ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the Garage Hub mobile application.

Information We Collect

Garage Hub may collect the following information:

  • Name and contact information
  • Phone number and email address
  • Vehicle information
  • Garage booking details
  • Payment and transaction information
  • Service and invoice information
  • Information required to provide app functionality

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Process garage service bookings
  • Send booking and service notifications
  • Provide invoices and service bills
  • Process payments and booking fees
  • Improve and maintain the Garage Hub application
  • Provide customer support

Payments

Payments made through Garage Hub may be processed by third-party payment service providers. We do not directly store your complete credit or debit card details.

Data Sharing

We may share necessary information with participating garages, payment service providers, cloud storage providers, and other service providers required to operate Garage Hub. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Children's Privacy

Garage Hub is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, you can contact us through the contact information provided in the Garage Hub application.
